HEALTH POLICY & MANAGEMENT

Sixian Du.

Understanding how policy shapes care.

I am a master’s student in Health Policy and Management at Huazhong University of Science and Technology. My research examines healthcare payment reform, hospital-at-home services, and the organisation of care for ageing populations.

I combine policy evaluation with patient and community perspectives, using methods such as interrupted time series, difference-in-differences, synthetic control and discrete choice experiments.
